Design preview

A clickable mockup of the planned app, built from the liquid-glass references you shared. It runs in the browser with sample dogs and a simulated walk. It is not the app: GPS, maps, weather, and saving all come in the real build.

  1. Tap Start. The walk is simulated and runs 6 times faster than real time.
  2. Quick Log: press and hold, slide into Nature or Behavior, keep sliding onto an event, let go. Push further out (or hold still) on a behavior for Dog, Human, or Other.
  3. Timed Log (beside it): same gesture, but letting go starts a timer. Tap End on the timer when the behavior stops. Quick Log keeps working meanwhile.
  4. Switch Beginner / Professional logging below, or from the avatar on Home.
  5. Hold End to finish. On the report, Understand My Dog suggests grouping related behaviors and offers a short, optional review. The sample report has an encounter ready to group.
