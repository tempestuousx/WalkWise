Design preview

A clickable mockup of the planned app, built from the liquid-glass references you shared. It runs in the browser with sample dogs and a simulated walk. It is not the app: GPS, maps, weather, and saving all come in the real build.

  1. Tap Start. The walk is simulated and runs 6 times faster than real time.
  2. Press and hold Quick Log, slide up into a category, keep sliding outward onto an event, and let go to log it.
  3. On a Behavior or Training event, hold still for a moment to get the trigger ring. Drag down to cancel.
  4. Tap Quick Log instead to use the tap-only menu.
  5. Hold End to finish. With no dog assigned you get the "Who did you walk?" step, then the report card.
